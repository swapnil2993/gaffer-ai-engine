# Football Hackers - LLM Context Pack

## Core Philosophy
Football success comes from accumulating many small, measurable advantages rather than relying on intuition, reputation, or isolated moments.

## 1. Process Over Results
- A single match rarely reveals true team quality.
- Results contain substantial randomness.
- Performance indicators are more predictive than scorelines.
- Evaluate process before outcome.

## 2. Variance
- Football is a low-scoring sport.
- Small events disproportionately affect outcomes.
- Luck is measurable.
- Long-term performance matters more than short-term results.

## 3. Cognitive Bias
- Experts disagree frequently.
- Observers remember outcomes.
- Observers overweight memorable events.
- Confirmation bias affects tactical analysis.

## 4. Market Efficiency
- Markets aggregate information.
- Prices often reveal hidden truths.
- Consensus can outperform expert opinion.

## 5. Edge Finding
- Search where others are not looking.
- Exploit undervalued areas.
- Advantages disappear once adopted widely.

Examples:
- Set pieces
- Throw-ins
- Recruitment
- Positional data
- Cognitive training

## 6. Repeatable Advantages
- Open play is chaotic.
- Set pieces are controlled situations.
- Controlled situations can be optimized.

## 7. Resource Asymmetry
- Wealth predicts success strongly.
- Smaller clubs cannot compete financially.
- Smaller clubs must innovate instead.

## 8. Tactical Evolution
Stages:
1. Innovation
2. Adoption
3. Mainstream acceptance
4. Competitive parity

## 9. Visibility and Feedback
- What can be measured can be improved.
- Video enabled systematic analysis.
- Analysis scales coaching knowledge.
- Faster feedback produces faster improvement.

## 10. Scouting as Prediction
- Identify future value.
- Ignore reputation.
- Search for transferable traits.
- Focus on projection.

A scout's job is not to identify who is good today.
A scout's job is to identify who will be good tomorrow.

## 11. Tactical Fit
- Player value is system dependent.
- Team context changes performance.
- Good players can fail in poor fits.

## 12. Cognitive Football
- Elite players perceive faster.
- Elite players process faster.
- Elite players decide faster.

Football intelligence is the ability to recognize patterns and select effective actions under time pressure.

## 13. Collective Intelligence
- Coordination beats isolated brilliance.
- Shared understanding accelerates decisions.
- Systems reduce cognitive load.

## 14. Football as a Network
- Players influence each other.
- Team performance is emergent.
- Relationships matter as much as individuals.

## LLM Operational Rules

### Team Analysis
1. Evaluate xG before goals.
2. Evaluate chance creation before scoreline.
3. Evaluate defensive prevention before goals conceded.
4. Look for patterns across multiple matches.
5. Treat individual matches as noisy samples.

### Recruitment Analysis
1. Prioritize tactical fit.
2. Project future value.
3. Ignore reputation bias.
4. Search for undervalued traits.
5. Consider system compatibility.

### Tactical Analysis
1. Prioritize line-breaking actions.
2. Prioritize territorial gain.
3. Identify repeatable advantages.
4. Evaluate collective structures.
5. Focus on decision-making speed.

### Performance Review
1. Separate process from outcome.
2. Separate luck from skill.
3. Separate reputation from performance.
4. Separate individual quality from system effects.
